# Third-party assets

## 3D models

The low-poly vehicle and tree models are adapted from
[Kenney Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit), released under CC0 1.0.
The complete license text is stored under `static/models/licenses/`.

## HDRI environment

The daytime environment uses `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky),
released under CC0 1.0.

The bundled asset is the 1K HDR edition and is used for background and
image-based lighting.
