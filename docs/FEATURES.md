# Wayline World features

## World

- daylit low-poly mountain island;
- continuous curved road with shoulders, road edges, and roadside reflectors;
- seated vehicle constraints and surface-height sampling;
- free driving, acceleration, turning, camera orbit, and touch controls;
- responsive quality tiers for mobile and low-core devices.

## Content timelines

- archive timeline generated from Hugo posts;
- gallery timeline generated from a dedicated gallery section;
- resume timeline generated from structured page front matter;
- click-to-read markers with contextual title cards;
- direct reader opening without leaving the 3D world.

## Reading and rewards

- in-world article reader;
- reading-progress tracking;
- `Q` / `Escape` reader exit;
- tag-based badge rewards;
- deduplicated local badge collection.

## Theme integration

- the home page is the full-screen world itself;
- the home world cannot be closed by UI, `Escape`, or backdrop click;
- normal Hugo pages remain available as content pages and secondary entry points;
- configurable world labels and intro copy;
- independent assets packaged under the theme itself.
